Frostline Hold

Hold a frozen fortress in a two-tone engraving style: build towers, upgrade them, call in a hero, and stop wave after wave.

  • 22 levels across 8 chapters, from Frostline Pass to the Rime Citadel, on a campaign map you drag like a long scroll.
  • 7 towers, each with two upgrade paths: Guard, Frost, Siege, Storm, Rune, Barracks and Ember.
  • 15 enemies, champions that shrug off crowd control, and two bosses: the Mine Wyrm and the Frost King.
  • The Warden, a hero you summon onto the field, with a frost nova.
  • Played with one finger or the mouse. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, works offline.
  • English and Simplified Chinese.

Available for macOS, Windows, Android, the web and the ESP-Mosaico handheld. Downloads: GitHub Releases.

霜线要塞

用双色版画风格守住冰原上的要塞：建塔、升级、召唤英雄，挡住一波又一波的怪物。

  • 22 个关卡：8 个章节从霜线山口一路打到冰封要塞，战役长卷可以左右拖动。
  • 7 种防御塔，每种都有两条升级路线：箭塔、冰霜塔、投石塔、雷暴塔、符文塔、盾卫营、余烬塔。
  • 15 种敌人，不受控制的精英，以及两个首领：矿洞巨蠕和冰封之王。
  • 英雄霜卫：召唤上场，释放冰霜新星。
  • 单指或鼠标即可游玩。没有广告、没有内购、不需要账号，可离线运行。
  • 支持简体中文和英文。

支持 macOS、Windows、Android、网页和 ESP-Mosaico 掌机。 下载：GitHub Releases。
