Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy covers the game Frostline Hold (霜线要塞), made by hkgood (rocky liu), on every platform it is published for: macOS, Windows, Android, the web and the ESP-Mosaico handheld.

The short version

Frostline Hold does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the game itself never connects to the internet. The Android version does not even request the network permission.

What stays on your device

To remember your campaign, the game keeps one small save file on your device. It holds the stars and best score of each level, which enemies you have already been introduced to, and your settings (music and sound volume, haptics, language, day or night theme). Nothing in it identifies you, and it never leaves your device through the game. Uninstalling the game, or deleting its data folder, removes it.

The platforms you play on

  • Stores. If you buy the game on the App Store, Google Play, Steam or itch.io, the store handles the purchase under its own privacy policy. We receive only the anonymous, aggregated sales and download reports every developer gets, never your name, email or payment details.
  • Backups and cloud saves. Android's own backup (when you have turned it on) and Steam Cloud (on Steam) may copy the save file to your Google or Steam account. These are features of your system and your account; we cannot see or reach those copies.
  • The web version. The browser keeps the save file in its local storage (IndexedDB) on your computer. The site that hosts the page (such as itch.io) has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: rocky.hk@gmail.com, or an issue on GitHub.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日

本政策适用于 hkgood（rocky liu）开发的游戏《霜线要塞》（Frostline Hold）在所有平台上的版本：macOS、Windows、Android、网页以及 ESP-Mosaico 掌机。

简要说明

《霜线要塞》不收集、不上传、不出售、也不分享任何个人数据。游戏没有账号系统、没有广告、没有数据统计、没有追踪，游戏本身也从不联网。Android 版甚至没有申请网络权限。

保存在你设备上的内容

为了记住你的战役进度，游戏会在你的设备上保存一个很小的存档文件，内容只有：每个关卡获得的星数和最高分、已经认识过的敌人，以及你的设置（音乐和音效音量、震动、语言、白天或夜间主题）。其中没有任何能识别你身份的信息，游戏也不会把它传出你的设备。卸载游戏或删除它的数据文件夹，存档就会一并删除。

你所使用的平台

  • 商店：如果你在 App Store、Google Play、Steam 或 itch.io 购买本游戏，购买过程由该商店按其自身的隐私政策处理。我们只能看到每位开发者都会收到的匿名汇总销售和下载报告，看不到你的姓名、邮箱或支付信息。
  • 备份和云存档：Android 系统备份（在你开启时）和 Steam 云存档（Steam 版）可能会把存档文件复制到你的 Google 或 Steam 账号中。这些是系统和账号自带的功能，我们无法查看或访问这些副本。
  • 网页版：浏览器会把存档保存在你电脑上的本地存储（IndexedDB）里。托管网页的网站（如 itch.io）有其自己的隐私政策。

儿童

本游戏不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

政策变更

如果本政策有变化，新版本会发布在本页面，并更新生效日期。

联系方式
